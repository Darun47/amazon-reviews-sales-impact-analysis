# Project Overview
In this project, the reviews of Amazon products are analyzed in order to comprehend the impact of customer rating, sentiment, and helpfulness on consumer trust and purchasing decision. This project aims to convert raw data in the form of review into significant insights by creating an interactive tableau dashboard.
This dashboard assists the stakeholders in knowing the customer satisfaction, review patterns as well as the performance of products. It facilitates evidence-based decision-making to enhance the quality of products and customer experience.

# Purpose of the Project
This project is primarily aimed to:

Examine customer rating (1-5 stars)

Detect favorable, neutral, negative sentiment of the reviews.

Learn the effects of positive reviews on customer confidence.

Display the changes in customer reviews over time.

Current knowledge in an interactive Tableau dashboard.

# Target Audience

The design of this dashboard is intended:

E-commerce managers

Marketing teams

Product managers

Business analysts

These insights help them to enhance customer satisfaction and products.

# Dataset Summary

Dataset: Amazon Reviews product Data.
Total Records: 568,454 reviews

Key columns used:

ProductId

Score (Rating)

Time (Review date)

Helpfulness votes

# Key Insights

Insight 1: Majority of the reviews are favorable.
Over 70 percent of customer ratings are 4 and 5 stars. This indicates that majority of customers are contented with the products.

Insight 2: The more helpful votes are obtained by higher ratings.
The reviews that are rated higher tend to have high helpfulness ratios. This implies that the customers have more confidence in favorable reviews.

Insight 3: Customer review activity grew with the time.
The reviews began to become large in the number. This demonstrates increased customer activity on online shopping.

Insight 4: Helpfulness ratio is used to find trusted reviews.
The reviews that have higher ratios of helpfulness are more beneficial to customers. These critiques have a very powerful impact on purchase decisions.
